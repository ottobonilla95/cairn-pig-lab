# Pig game rules

- Two players take turns. The first to reach 100 banked points wins.
- Rolling 2–6 adds that value to the current turn's unbanked points.
- A player can hold to bank those points and end the turn.
- Rolling 1 loses the current turn's unbanked points and ends the turn. Previously banked points are kept.
- If the banked score plus the current turn's points reaches 100, those points are banked immediately and the player wins.
- The baseline opponent holds once its current turn reaches at least 10 points.
